About Positive AI

Making mental wellbeing as valued as physical health

Positive AI Innovations Private Limited is a technology company building AI that makes mental health support accessible, personalised and proactive — so people can act early, without fear of judgement.

Our vision

Revolutionise wellbeing by putting mental health first

To revolutionise wellbeing by prioritising mental health through AI-driven innovation, empowering individuals to build confidence, enhance productivity and achieve lasting happiness.

Our mission

Break the stigma, detect early, care personally

To use AI to break down the stigma around mental health, provide early detection tools and offer personalised interventions — so mental wellness is valued as much as physical health and people can live fulfilling lives.

The problem

Four barriers stand between people and care

Overlooked

Mental health is neglected in favour of physical health, despite rising anxiety, stress and depression.

Stigma

Fear of judgement and few accessible resources stop people seeking early diagnosis and support.

Under-treated

1 in 5 people in India experience a mental health issue; only 10–12% receive the help they need.

Out of reach

60% of people in rural areas lack access to professional support, so intervention comes too late.

Our approach

What we bring that others don't

AI-driven early detection

Algorithms that pick up early signs of anxiety, depression and stress, so people can act before symptoms worsen.

Personalised interventions

Care plans that recommend targeted therapies and self-care practices based on each person's needs — and adapt as they change.

Real-time support

Continuous check-ins and timely guidance, so help is there in the moments that matter most.

One complete, stigma-free ecosystem

Where others offer a chatbot or a therapy marketplace alone, we combine detection, self-help, companionship and expert care in one seamless journey.
